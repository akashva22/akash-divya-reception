FLAT VERSION - all files live in ONE place (no folders). Upload every file to the same level on GitHub.
  index.html   page      style.css   colours/fonts      main.js   countdown & effects
  bgm.js       music     content.js  ALL editable text
Your own music (.mp3) and couple photo go in the same place; then set bgm.file / photo in content.js.

LINK PREVIEW: preview.jpg is the thumbnail shown when the link is shared. The title/description for it are in the <meta> tags at the top of index.html (edit them there if the date or venue changes).


PHOTO SLIDESHOW: put your pre-wedding photos in the photos/ folder named 1.jpg, 2.jpg, 3.jpg ... (in order, no gaps). They are picked up automatically and play after the guest scrolls down. The 3 files provided are placeholders: replace them with your own. Tip: resize each to about 1600px wide and under 500 KB.
